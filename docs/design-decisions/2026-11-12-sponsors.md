# Sponsors on spectator pages (2026-11-12)

Owner request: sponsorship on public pages for band contests, choir and group festivals, and school fairs, as an
add-on, on the spectator-facing pages only (not volunteer sign-up, not registration).

Owner decisions before mock-ups:

- **An add-on we switch on per account:** a Field Command admin turns Sponsors on for an account in the admin pages.
  Organizers without it never see sponsor settings.
- **The organizer's own sponsors, paid outside the app:** boosters and PTOs thank the local businesses they've already
  sold sponsorships to. The organizer adds each one; no money goes through the app. Field Command never sells or picks
  sponsors, and nothing tracks families (the privacy page's "we never show ads" gets a small wording update to say so).
- **Where:** a "Thank you to our sponsors" section on the public event page; a "Presented by" line under the event's
  title for one title sponsor; and a small sponsor line on the live day views families watch (band contest live
  schedule and results, festival Happening now, fair map and zones). Never on volunteer sign-up, registration pages,
  or emails.
- **Levels the organizer names:** up to 4 levels, named and ordered by the organizer (Gold, Silver, Bronze, Friend);
  higher levels get bigger logos and appear first.
