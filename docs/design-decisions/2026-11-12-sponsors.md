# Sponsors on spectator pages (2026-11-12)

Owner request: sponsorship on public pages for band contests, choir and group festivals, and school fairs, as an
add-on, on the spectator-facing pages only (not volunteer sign-up, not registration).

Owner decisions before mock-ups:

- **An add-on we switch on per account:** a Field Command admin turns Sponsors on for an account in the admin pages.
  Organizers without it never see sponsor settings.
- **The organizer's own sponsors, paid outside the app:** boosters and PTOs thank the local businesses they've already
  sold sponsorships to. The organizer adds each one; no money goes through the app. Field Command never sells or picks
  sponsors, and nothing tracks families (the privacy page's "we never show ads" gets a small wording update to say so).
- **Where:** a "Thank you to our sponsors" section on the public event page; a "Presented by" line under the event's
  title for one title sponsor; and a small sponsor line on the live day views families watch (band contest live
  schedule and results, festival Happening now, fair map and zones). Never on volunteer sign-up, registration pages,
  or emails.
- **Levels the organizer names:** up to 4 levels, named and ordered by the organizer (Gold, Silver, Bronze, Friend);
  higher levels get bigger logos and appear first.

Next: mock-ups, then a panel review.

## Panel review of the mock-ups (2026-11-12)

Report: `docs/design-reviews/2026-11-12-sponsors.md`. Verdict: build with fixes. Owner decisions:

- **Sponsor thank-yous aren't ads; say so.** Keep "we never show ads" and add one plain line wherever it's promised:
  organizers can thank their own sponsors on public event pages, never on sign-up or registration pages or in emails;
  we never sell or place them; nothing tracks who sees them. A "Sponsors on event pages" section on the privacy page.
  Every "ads" claim in the site and `docs/brand-voice.md` is reworded to match.
- **Fairs: one combined section.** "Thank you to our sponsors and partners": sponsors by level with logos first,
  then community partners as a text list; a business that's both shows once, as a sponsor.
- **Hidden sponsors and undo.** Each sponsor has "Show on the public page" (on by default; "9 showing, 2 hidden"),
  and Remove has a short Undo.
- **Everything else as the panel suggested**, including:
  - Logos resized on upload, cached, and readable at every level; names never cut off (text under small logos).
  - Sponsors only on the main public page, enforced in code and checked; the add-on enforced in the database.
  - Drafts: the Sponsors page works, "Shows when you publish"; sponsors stay after the event; the live line ends
    with the day.
  - Add-on off: organizers see a line saying sponsors are off and kept; the admin dialog names upcoming events.
  - "Add a sponsor" at the top of the page; levels created from the add window; no Level field with no levels (the
    first level takes existing sponsors); remove a level by choosing where its sponsors go; arrows move across levels.
  - "Add from your other events" handles duplicates, level names, and the 4-level limit; copies logos.
  - Safe links (https only, no tracking parameters, `rel="sponsored noopener noreferrer"`), no click counts.
  - Copying last year's contest or festival doesn't bring sponsors; the Sponsors page offers them instead.
  - "Presented by" stays on the live day, as a link with an underline, loaded only on the main public page.
  - What each level gets is said on the Sponsors page; the "N more" link is a real tap target; dark mode tiles
    don't glare (name-only tiles follow the theme).
  - Website: only a question "Can we thank our sponsors?" answered "Ask us when you join", not a priced add-on;
    fix today's fair line that promises sponsors.

Next: round 2 mock-ups, a light check, then build.
