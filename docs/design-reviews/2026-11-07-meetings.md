**Coverage:** every reviewer opened the screenshots their role requires. The UI Designer and the
consultant opened all 102. Five reviewers noted that the phone shots of both emails (14 and 15) stop
before the "Change or cancel" and "Open your Meetings page" buttons, so nobody could check those
buttons at phone width. That is the preview frame, not a known problem in the email, but it should be
re-shot.

**Shared with conferences:** the setup page, the list of meetings, the booking page, the change-or-cancel
page, and the booking emails are the same pages for Meetings and Parent-teacher conferences. Each
finding below says whether its fix would also change conferences.

## The consultant's verdict

**Fix first, then build on it.** Meetings is a solid feature: the booking page is short, needs no
account, and asks things in the order a parent thinks of them, and the emails and the change-or-cancel
page put when and where first. Two things would cause real failures: the "I'll add it to each invite"
video option lets a video meeting go ahead with no link, with nothing tracking whether one was sent,
and the "You're booked" screen doesn't say where to go. The rest is polish: one page with several
names, conference words that leaked into Meetings, and some dark-mode emphasis that flips. **The one
change that would help most:** make one pasted video link the default and recommended way, and if
someone does choose a link for each meeting, show "Video link: not sent yet" on each video meeting in
Your meetings, with a box to paste the link so the app emails it.

## Blocks people (severity 3–4)

Nothing here gets built or shipped until the owner decides.

### 1. A video meeting can go ahead with no link, and nothing catches it · severity 3 · found by 6 of 8
The other two reviewers (UX Content Manager and Marketing Manager) flagged the related wording.

- **Where:** 03 Where you'll meet, "How do people get the link?", with "I'll add it to each invite"
  selected (`src/app/dashboard/events/[eventId]/conferences/conference-forms.tsx:766-795`); the
  organizer's booking email line "You send them your video link" (`src/lib/conference-bookings.ts:428`);
  04 Your meetings, which has no link status; 09 and 10, the Video choice ("Join online.")
- **What happens:**
  - The calendar invite comes from the app, not from the organizer. Adding a Zoom link to her own
    calendar entry never reaches the person who booked, so the only way to do it is to email each person
    by hand. We checked this in the code.
  - The person booking is told the link "will come in a calendar invite before your meeting." The booking
    page itself says only "Join online."
  - Nothing on Your meetings shows which video meetings still need a link, the organizer's email doesn't
    include the person's email address, and nothing reminds her before the day. In the sample, 5 of the 9
    bookings are video.
  - The same tab recommends "one meeting for the whole time" with a waiting room, which only works with
    "Paste it here," yet the other option is selected in the sample.
- **Who it hurts:** organizer at a laptop and PTO president (a hidden step they can't see or check);
  teacher with a 30-minute lunch (many invites to send by hand); volunteer parent and band director (sit at
  meeting time with no link)
- **Standard:** error prevention, visibility of system status, match with the real world
- **Options the reviewers proposed:**
  - **A. Remove the option** (Product Owner, Software Engineer). When Video is checked, require one pasted
    link, with the waiting-room guide as it is now. Simplest, and fits the request to start smaller.
  - **B. Keep it, but make it safe** (consultant, Product Manager, UX Designer, Software Engineer). Make
    "Paste it here" the default and mark it recommended. Rename the other "I'll email each person a link
    myself." On Your meetings, show "Video link: not sent yet" on each video meeting, with a box to paste
    the link so the app emails it (or a "Mark link sent" switch). Add the person's email to the organizer's
    booking email, list missing links in a day-before note, and change the booking page's Video hint to
    "Dana Whitfield emails you the link before your meeting."
- **Recommendation:** A for now. It is small, and B can come later if pilot organizers ask for it.
- **Also changes conferences:** yes. The same option, setup page, and emails serve conferences, so either
  fix reaches teachers too, unless it's limited to Meetings.
- **Decision:** _Take the advice / Fix it differently / Keep as is (reason)_

### 2. Changing the meeting length, hours, or breaks after people book quietly breaks their meetings · severity 3 · found by 2 of 8
- **Where:** 02 Meeting times, "Each meeting," "Time between meetings," "Change the day or hours," and
  "Add a break" (`src/app/dashboard/events/[eventId]/conferences/actions.ts:52-184`)
- **What happens:**
  - Only switching between one day and every week is blocked once people have booked. Length, gaps, hours,
    and breaks save freely with 9 people booked. We checked this in the code.
  - A booking doesn't keep its own length. If Dana changes 15 minutes to 30, Maria's 9:00 meeting becomes
    9:00 to 9:30 on Dana's list and overlaps James at 9:15, while Maria's calendar invite still says 9:15.
    No one is emailed.
  - Shortening the hours, or adding a break over a booked time, leaves those meetings outside the hours
    with no warning. The screen still says "All changes saved."
- **Who it hurts:** organizer at a laptop and PTO president (think everything is fine); volunteer parent
  (turns up at a time the organizer no longer expects)
- **Standard:** error prevention, visibility of system status
- **Suggested fix:**
  - Each booking keeps its own length from the moment it's made.
  - Before saving, check for booked meetings that would overlap, fall outside the new hours, or land in a
    break, and name them: "2 booked meetings fall outside your new hours: Rachel Green 11:15, Marcus
    Johnson 11:30. They stay booked. Reschedule or cancel them on Your meetings."
  - The Product Owner's simpler option: once anyone has booked, lock the length and gaps the same way
    "Every week" is locked.
  - This is a database change, so it needs a matching privacy and safety test.
- **Also changes conferences:** yes. The same setup page and booking rules serve conferences, which have
  the same problem today.
- **Decision:** _Take the advice / Fix it differently / Keep as is (reason)_

### 3. "Every week" is locked once anyone books, and the only way out is canceling everyone · severity 3 · found by 4 of 8
- **Where:** 02 Meeting times, the greyed "Every week" card: "People have booked. Cancel their meetings on
  your Meetings page to switch." (`src/app/dashboard/events/[eventId]/conferences/conference-forms.tsx:259`)
- **What happens:**
  - With 9 of 12 times booked, the feature is working, and the organizer may want the same hours every
    week. The screen's only suggestion is to cancel 9 people first, and it doesn't say how many.
  - Nothing offers another day or a copy of the event.
  - The reason is in faded grey, measured at 2.7 to 1 contrast, though it's the only instruction there.
- **Who it hurts:** organizer at a laptop and PTO president (stuck just when it's working); front office
  administrator (fields calls from canceled people)
- **Standard:** user control and freedom, error prevention; text contrast (WCAG 1.4.3, in spirit)
- **Reviewers disagree:** the Product Manager rated it 3; the UX Designer and UI Designer rated it 2; the
  UX Content Manager rated the wording 1. The Product Owner, Software Engineer, and consultant listed the
  lock itself as working well, since it explains in place why the option is off.
- **Options the reviewers proposed:**
  - **A. Let the switch go ahead and keep the bookings** (Product Manager, UX Designer): the booked day
    becomes the first week. Bigger build.
  - **B. Keep the lock, but give a forward path** (Product Manager, UX Designer, UX Content Manager): "Need
    more times? Make a weekly copy" (copies the setup without the bookings), say how many have booked, link
    to Your meetings, and show the reason in readable text. Never suggest canceling people to change a
    setting.
- **Recommendation:** B. The lock protects people's bookings; only the way out needs to change.
- **Also changes conferences:** yes. Conferences use the same card ("Families have booked…").
- **Decision:** _Take the advice / Fix it differently / Keep as is (reason)_

### 4. The guest's cancel button says "Continue" · severity 3 (consultant: 2) · found by 6 of 8
- **Where:** 13 cancel booking confirm, the window's red button
  (`src/app/e/[slug]/book/r/[token]/manage-forms.tsx:101-108`, which falls back to "Continue" in
  `src/components/confirm-dialog.tsx:227`); the button that opens it reads "Cancel Meeting" in title case
- **What happens:** the window asks "Cancel the meeting?" with "Never mind" and a red "Continue."
  "Continue" could mean carry on with the meeting or carry on canceling. Canceling can't be undone, and the
  person's details are deleted at once. The organizer's own window gets this right ("Keep the meeting" and
  "Cancel and email them").
- **Who it hurts:** volunteer parent on a phone and PTO president in the car line (one-handed, distracted,
  may cancel by mistake or keep a time they meant to give up)
- **Standard:** buttons say what happens; error prevention
- **Ratings:** UX Content Manager 3; consultant, UX Designer, and UI Designer 2; Product Owner and Marketing
  Manager 1. The consultant gave no separate reason for 2, so both are shown.
- **Suggested fix:** title "Cancel your meeting?", body "Your 11:00 AM time opens up for someone else.
  We'll email everyone on this booking, and your details are deleted," buttons "Keep my meeting" and
  "Cancel my meeting." Change "Cancel Meeting" to "Cancel meeting" (and "Cancel conference").
- **Also changes conferences:** yes, the same window serves both. A small wording fix for both is the
  right call.
- **Decision:** _Take the advice / Fix it differently / Keep as is (reason)_

### 5. A smaller first version, and a website that matches it · severity 3 · found by 2 of 8
- **Where:** the whole flow, and 16 the website page (`src/app/meetings/page.tsx`)
- **What happens:** you asked about starting smaller. Today Meetings covers one day or every week; in
  person, video, or phone; two ways to send a video link; up to three extra people; rescheduling to any
  typed-in time; breaks; Met/No-show; and a printable summary. The Where you'll meet tab alone is a long
  form with nested choices, which cuts against "Set up in minutes" on the website. The website also
  promises weekly hours, days off, one upcoming meeting per person, and a month calendar.
- **Correction:** the Product Owner was unsure whether those website promises are built. We checked: they
  exist in the code. They just weren't part of this review, so nobody has checked them on screen.
- **Who it hurts:** organizer at a laptop and teacher with a 30-minute lunch (more decisions up front);
  you, if a promise on the website doesn't hold up
- **Standard:** brand voice "Promises we keep"; minimalist design
- **Ratings:** Product Owner 3, Product Manager 2. The consultant didn't raise it.
- **Options the reviewers proposed:**
  - **A. Smallest complete version** (Product Owner): one day only; in person and video with one pasted
    link; the person plus up to three others; organizer reschedules to open times only; both sides can
    cancel; a printable list for the day; booked, reminder, moved, and canceled emails. Then rewrite the
    website to match and re-shoot its pictures from the one-day sample.
  - **B. Keep it all, but tuck the extras away** (Product Manager): show weekly, phone calls, and "I'll add
    it to each invite" behind "More options," and fold the Zoom and Google Meet steps into one "How to set
    up a waiting room" link.
- **Recommendation:** this is a product decision for you, not a usability fault. If you want to start
  smaller, B is less work than A and keeps what's built.
- **Also changes conferences:** no, if the options are hidden for Meetings only.
- **Decision:** _Take the advice / Fix it differently / Keep as is (reason)_

## Should fix (severity 2)

**After booking, and the booking page**
- "You're booked" doesn't say how or where (no "In person, Suite 200…" or "Video"), and the header above
  still says "In person or video. You choose below" with nothing below. Reuse the block from the
  change-or-cancel page, and add an "Add to calendar" button (7 of 8; conferences too)
- The Video choice says only "Join online." Say how the link arrives (3 of 8; conferences too)
- The Book button is a small dashed outline until the form is complete, with no reason given, and the
  disabled look differs from screen to screen. Use one disabled style, a "Pick a time and add your name and
  email" hint, and a full-width button on phones (1 of 8, UI Designer; conferences too)
- The booking page doesn't say who's behind it. Add "with Dana Whitfield · Cedar Ridge Boosters," as the
  email already does (1 of 8, Marketing Manager)
- "We'll remind you the day before" isn't true for bookings made less than a day ahead, and a very busy day
  could skip some reminders for good. Say "this email is your reminder" for late bookings, and run the
  reminders more often (1 of 8, Software Engineer; conferences too)
- "Every time is booked" tells people to contact Dana but gives no way to. Add an email link (2 of 8;
  conferences too). The Product Owner thought this state and "someone took your time while you were filling
  in the form" had no design; both are built, but weren't photographed, so they should be

**One page, several names**
- Your meetings is also called "See your meetings," "Meetings list," and "Meetings page," and "Manage
  meetings" opens setup, not the list (7 of 8; conferences too). Six reviewers suggest "Your meetings" for
  the list and "Meeting setup" for settings; the Marketing Manager suggests "Meetings page" everywhere.
  **Recommendation:** "Your meetings" and "Meeting setup," with "Your meetings" as the main button once
  people have booked

**The organizer's booking email**
- The subject doesn't say who booked, the body has no email, phone, or note, and "mark it Met or No-show"
  arrives days early (5 of 8; conferences too). The Software Engineer praised leaving contact details out,
  so a shared calendar doesn't show them. **Recommendation:** add them to the email body only and keep the
  calendar entry short, which answers both. Move the Met/No-show line to the reminder

**Promises and privacy wording**
- The privacy note when creating the event says "seen only by you," while every other screen says "you
  and your co-hosts" (2 of 8; the conference note has the same mistake)
- Public pages and the guest email say "co-hosts," an in-app permission name. Use "Dana Whitfield and the
  people helping run these meetings" (3 of 8; conferences too)
- "It comes off their calendar" and "so it's on your calendar" promise more than an attached invite does.
  Use "with an update for their calendar" and "add it to your calendar with the attached invite" (2 of 8;
  conferences too)
- The Conferences card says "Free during the pilot," but the Meetings card doesn't, and nothing says what
  happens after the pilot ends on January 1 (3 of 8)
- Every email footer says "Run event day from one place," not the tagline. Changing the tagline in the one
  place it's set fixes every email, conferences included (4 of 8)

**Choosing and creating the event**
- The Meetings and Parent-teacher conferences cards read almost the same. Lead each with who it's for, and
  put them side by side (3 of 8)
- The new-event form has a "When" box with no field that looks like one, and the time-zone hint starts
  "Where you'll meet." Remove the box, and start the hint "The time zone where you'll meet" (6 of 8)

**The setup page**
- The save bar stays pinned to the bottom of the screen even when nothing needs saving, and a field you tab
  to can end up under it. Show the full bar only when there are unsaved changes, and keep focused fields
  clear of it (4 of 8; conferences too). Correction: in the screenshots it looks like it covers "Change the
  day or hours" on desktop. That's how the full-page capture works, not what people see while scrolling
- On phones, the second tab is cut off ("Where you'll m…") with no sign the row scrolls. Stack the tabs
  (2 of 8; conferences too)
- "Unpublish" sits right next to everyday buttons of different sizes, and its window doesn't say what
  happens to the 9 people already booked. Separate it, and say so (3 of 8; conferences too)
- The required "add a link to each invite" note is the plainest thing on the tab, while an optional tip
  gets a tinted panel (1 of 8, UI Designer; moot if finding 1 removes that option)
- Conference words leak into Meetings: "Conference day," "from your first conference to your last,"
  "Dinner" breaks on a morning event, a "staff meeting at 3:30" example, and "We emailed the family" and
  "for your teacher to call" in messages (5 of 8)

**Your meetings and the organizer's windows**
- In dark mode, "Cancel meeting" is the brightest link on each card, brighter than "Reschedule." Dim it in
  both modes (2 of 8; conferences too)
- In dark mode, empty radio buttons and checkboxes look filled, so they read as selected (2 of 8;
  conferences too)
- Open times are a dead end: the organizer can't book someone who calls or stops by. Add "Book someone" on
  each open row (1 of 8, UX Designer)
- "Type in a time" when rescheduling allows any time on the day with no word about limits or overlaps, and
  the organizer can't change in person or video there (1 of 8, Product Owner)
- When the organizer cancels, the person's details are deleted at once, with no undo and no record. Keep a
  collapsed "Canceled" list until the 30-day deletion, and offer Undo for a few seconds (1 of 8, Product
  Manager)
- The organizer's cancel deletes the booking before the emails go out, so if sending fails, "please let
  them know" leaves her with no way to reach them. Put their contact details in that message, or delete
  only after sending (1 of 8, Software Engineer; conferences too)

**The printable summary**
- Printed before the day, it says "Not marked" nine times and "0 met · 0 no-shows," and has no column for
  in person or video, phone, or note. Before the day, show Time, Who, How, Phone, and Note, with a blank
  "Met?" column to tick (6 of 8; conferences too)

**The change-or-cancel page and links**
- People can't add or remove someone, fix their note, or see that they can reply to the email to reach the
  organizer (2 of 8; conferences too)
- Everyone on a booking shares one change-or-cancel link, so an extra person can cancel the whole meeting
  and sees the main person's note, and nothing records who made a change. Give each person their own link
  (1 of 8, Software Engineer; conferences too; a database change that needs a matching test)

**Safety**
- The public booking form can send email in the organizer's name to any address, the bot check is off
  until its keys are set, and "one meeting per person" is easy to get around with another email address.
  Make the bot check a launch requirement and limit bookings per device (1 of 8, Software Engineer;
  conferences too)

**The guest email**
- The address runs straight into the next sentence ("…TX 78701 Please wait outside the room"). Put them on
  separate lines (5 of 8; conferences too)

## Polish (severity 1)

- New-event heading "New meetings" reads as a plural noun; use "Set up meetings"
- Intro says "Just a name for now" but also asks for a time zone
- The reschedule and cancel windows say only "Maria Lopez sees it," not Jo Lopez too
- Change-or-cancel page doesn't name the event, and "Change your time" also covers how you meet
- "Links to share" shows a different link from the setup page for the same booking page, and labels it
  "People / Book a time"
- Open times wrap to "11:00 AM – 11:15 / AM" on Your meetings
- Phone windows: buttons are loose and shrink-wrapped; make them full width and stacked
- The success card looks like any other section; add a check mark and a success tint
- Copy link and QR code links are styled differently; "Save" and "Save meeting times" don't match
- The records note sits above the title on the printed summary; move it under the table
- "Optional." under Place name; use "Place name (optional)"
- Periods on fragments: "Join online.", "A single day of meetings.", "Meeting with Dana Whitfield."
- "Who's coming" is followed by "You, and anyone else coming"; the three-person limit isn't stated up front;
  "Meetings: 15 min each" means length
- Public page footer says "school event management for bands, choirs, PTOs, and front offices"; for Meetings,
  say what it is and link to the Meetings website page
- Email links break at the hyphen on phones
- The "Setup complete" panel shows on desktop but not on tablet or phone
- Website: three different styles for "Join the pilot"; "How it works" pictures cut off on tablet and bright
  white in dark mode; "who's now and who's next," "simply," and "book themselves" wording
- App menu says "Volunteers" on phone but "Volunteer tracking" on desktop (outside this review)
- Declining in a calendar app doesn't free the time; add a line to the invite saying to use the
  change-or-cancel link
- A typed-in reschedule and a new booking at the same moment could overlap
- Nothing measures whether Meetings is working (time to publish, share of times booked, cancels, no-shows,
  video meetings without a link)

## What's working well

- The booking page is one page, needs no account, shows only open times, and the button changes to "Book
  11:00 AM"
- "Add someone else," with "Each person gets the confirmation, calendar invite, and reminder," answers the
  obvious question at the right moment
- The organizer's reschedule and cancel windows name the person, list what will happen, offer an optional
  note ("Maria Lopez sees it… under your name"), and use buttons that say what they do
- The change-or-cancel page leads with when, where, and how to wait, and "Canceling frees your time for
  someone else" gives a kind reason to cancel instead of not showing up
- Setup shows the 12 actual meeting times as you change settings, the tabs show what's done, and "All
  changes saved" answers "is this saved?"
- Privacy notes are specific and honest, look the same everywhere, and say 30 days every time
- Times are always shown in one named time zone
- Behind the scenes: booking rules live in the database, double bookings are blocked, and calendar
  invites update and cancel correctly
- Dark mode text contrast is strong overall, and tap targets are at least 44 pixels
- The website follows the house structure: tagline, honest support language, "No ads, no spam," and a
  clear "Join the pilot" next step

## Acceptance criteria (from the Product Owner)

- Given a published one-day event, when a person books 11:00 in person, then the booked screen and email
  show the time, "In person," and the room and address, and 11:00 disappears from the booking page
- Given 11:00 is taken while someone is filling in the form, when they press "Book 11:00 AM," then they see
  that it was just taken, their details are kept, and the open times are listed
- Given every time is booked, when someone opens the link, then they see "All times are booked" and how to
  reach the organizer
- Given video is offered, when the organizer saves setup, then a meeting link is required, and every video
  booking's email contains it
- Given 9 bookings, when the organizer changes the meeting length, then they are told what happens to
  existing bookings before saving
- Given the organizer reschedules Maria Lopez, when they pick an open time, then Maria and Jo both get an
  updated invite, and the old time reopens
- Given a guest cancels from their link, when they confirm, then they see a "canceled" screen, the
  organizer gets an email that removes the meeting from their calendar, and reopening the link shows "This
  meeting was canceled"
- Given the day hasn't started, when the organizer prints the summary, then each row shows Time, Who, How,
  and Phone, with no Met column

## Limits

This is an AI review, not testing with real people. Before deciding on findings 1 to 5, try the flow with
two or three real people: an organizer setting up a video meeting, and a parent booking and then canceling
on a phone.
