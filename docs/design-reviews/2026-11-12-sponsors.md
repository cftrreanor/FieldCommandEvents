# Design review: Sponsors on spectator pages (mock-ups)

| | |
|---|---|
| **What was reviewed** | 8 mock-up boards (`docs/design-reviews/2026-11-12-sponsors-mockups/`): admin switch, the event page card, the Sponsors page, add/edit/remove/reuse windows, public pages (band contest, festival, fair), live views, states, and website and privacy wording |
| **Stage** | Mock-ups before building |
| **Size** | Full panel: all seven roles plus the consultant |
| **Date** | 2026-11-12 |

## The consultant's verdict

Build with fixes. Sponsors stay off sign-up, registration, and emails; the live line is quiet and below the live
information; reuse of last year's sponsors suits boosters. The one thing to do first: the owner decides whether an
organizer's own sponsor thank-you counts as an "ad", and every public promise is reworded to match.

## Blocks people (severity 3)

1. **The "no ads" promise** (8 of 8). Board 8 changes the privacy page's "never show ads" to "never sell ads",
   a quiet narrowing, and misses other claims: the site description (`src/app/layout.tsx`), the home page and its
   questions (`src/app/page.tsx`), the volunteers and visitor-day pages (`src/lib/audiences.ts`), and
   `docs/brand-voice.md`.
2. **Lower-level logos unreadable and names cut off** (Marketing at 3; 4 others at 1 to 2). A sponsor who sent a
   logo looks worse than one who didn't.
3. **Logos served full size** (Software Engineer). A page of 22 logos could be tens of megabytes on stadium signal;
   needs resizing on upload and long caching.
4. **Presented by leaking onto sign-up and registration** (Software Engineer). The shared page heading is used by
   those pages too; sponsors must load only on the main public page.
5. **The add-on switch enforced in the database** (Software Engineer), not only hidden.

## Should fix (severity 2)

- Unpublished events and after the event aren't designed; status words would be untrue on a draft (6 of 8).
- Fairs get two thank-you lists with the same names (7 of 8).
- No way to enter a sponsor before they've paid or sent a logo (hidden sponsors), and no undo for Remove (5 of 8).
- Turning the add-on off says nothing to the organizer and doesn't name upcoming events (5 of 8).
- The first sponsor with no levels, moving sponsors between levels, removing a level, and "Add from your other
  events" conflicts (duplicates, level names, the 4-level limit) aren't designed (4 of 8).
- The Sponsors page puts settings above "Add a sponsor"; the "7 more" link is small and low contrast in dark mode;
  the live line splits the schedule from Up next; white tiles glare in dark mode (4 of 8).
- Errors and uploads in the add window; a logo over 2 MB is a dead end (3 of 8).
- Link safety: https only, no tracking parameters, `rel="sponsored noopener noreferrer"`; logo files re-encoded;
  storage cleanup; reorder conflicts (Software Engineer).
- No way for an organizer to learn about or ask for the add-on; the website should say only what's true (3 of 8).
- What each level gets isn't said where organizers write sponsor packets (Marketing).
- Wording: one name for "Presented by", event-kind words, "Local businesses" claim, help text length.

## What's working well

The "where sponsors show / never show" box; nothing changes when there are no sponsors; the live line after the live
information; reuse of last year's sponsors; the tall-logo preview; calm confirmations; upload-only logos from our own
storage; the admin switch with who and when.

## Limits

An AI review, not testing with real people. Before launch, show a booster club treasurer and one sponsor the public
page.
